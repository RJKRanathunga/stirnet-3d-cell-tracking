# Validation plan

1. Establish unchanged controls: spheres, elongated ellipsoids, irregular
   single components, separated lobes, and arbitrary connected chains.
2. Evaluate unequal overlapping ellipsoids with anisotropic voxel spacing.
3. Review every applied supplemental marker together with its cap pair,
   cross-sections, area profile, ellipsoid fit, and unique support.
4. Review false negatives by rejection reason rather than lowering multiple
   gates at once.
5. Calibrate provisional physical thresholds on held-out curated real scenes.
6. Re-run Stage 4 compatibility and complete-mask/marker-ID invariants.
